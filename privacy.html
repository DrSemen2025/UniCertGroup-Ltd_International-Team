Website policy

Privacy notice.

A plain-language description of how this static website handles information.

Effective date: 20 July 2026. This notice should be reviewed if hosting, analytics or the enquiry workflow changes.

Website operation

This website is published using GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service. Refer to GitHub’s own privacy documentation for its platform practices.

No site analytics or advertising cookies

This version of the website does not install an analytics product, advertising tracker or marketing cookie. No consent banner is shown because the site does not intentionally set non-essential cookies.

Enquiry form

The contact form does not post data to UniCertGroup Ltd. or to a third-party form processor. It prepares a message and asks your device to open your email application. Until you choose to send that email, the website does not receive or store the form contents.

Email communication

If you send an email to service@unicertgroup.com, its content and addressing information will be processed for correspondence and project evaluation. Do not send export-controlled, classified or highly sensitive technical data before an appropriate exchange method and terms have been agreed.

Your choices

You may contact the team to request correction or deletion of information held in correspondence, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping obligations. The responsible legal entity, applicable law and retention terms should be confirmed in the contract or engagement documentation for a project.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to service@unicertgroup.com.
