Compliance & Documentation Readiness

The 2026–2028 Regulatory Window

The Machinery Regulation is not the first deadline. Two obligations bite before it, and one reaches equipment already installed in the EU. Countdowns below are computed live against today’s date.

G0

Regulatory exposure triage

2–3 working days · fixed fee · remote

One product family, one target market. Which instruments apply, which obligation lands first, who is the responsible economic operator in the Union, and is there a digital element — and therefore a CRA clock?

Deliverable: 4–6 page memo with an applicable-regulation map, an obligation-holder matrix and your own dates.
G1

Machinery documentation gap assessment

2–4 weeks · fixed fee

Document inventory, risk-assessment status, technical-file structure, declaration and manual review, drawing and calculation traceability, Machinery Regulation 2027 transition gaps clause by clause, and evidentiary readiness under the new liability regime.

Deliverable: Severity-ranked gap register with a remediation estimate per line. Remediation itself is quoted separately.
G2

CRA readiness for connected equipment

3–5 weeks · time-critical

Product inventory including the installed base, responsible-entity determination, reportability criteria, ENISA Single Reporting Platform onboarding, named submitters, and reporting templates mapped to the 24 h / 72 h / 14 d stages.

Deliverable: A reporting procedure your team can execute at 03:00. Excludes penetration testing and notified-body assessment.
G3

EU market-entry documentation programme

Phased · quoted per phase · after G1

Responsibility definition and authorised-representative support, technical-document architecture, missing engineering documentation, manuals and datasheets, drawing and calculation review, notified-body coordination, and CBAM data traceability.

Deliverable: Each phase is a separate order. Never quoted as one undefined “full EU market entry” package.
Scope boundary. Final conformity assessment and EU type-examination are performed by a notified body. Legal interpretation belongs to qualified counsel, cybersecurity testing to an accredited testing partner, and CBAM emissions verification to an accredited verifier. The EU Declaration of Conformity is signed by the manufacturer or its authorised representative — never by us. We prepare, structure and defend the documentation; we do not certify it.
