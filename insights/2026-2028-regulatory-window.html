Active phase

EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism

The definitive CBAM regime started in 2026. Importers of covered goods should determine whether they fall within scope, whether the annual mass-based threshold or a specific exception applies, and which authorization, emissions-data and certificate obligations affect the supply chain.

This is not a product certificate in the conventional conformity-assessment sense. It is included because documentation owners may need supplier and embedded-emissions data alongside market-access records.

Source: European Commission — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism.

11 September 2026

Cyber Resilience Act reporting obligations

Article 14 reporting obligations apply from 11 September 2026. Manufacturers of products with digital elements should define how actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents are identified, escalated, evidenced and reported within the applicable deadlines.

Documentation work should include responsible contacts, decision criteria, reporting records and coordination between product security, engineering, legal and customer-facing functions.

Source: European Commission — CRA reporting obligations. The Commission’s summary states the reporting start date and explains the phased deadlines.

Member-State transposition by 9 December 2026

Revised Product Liability Directive

Directive (EU) 2024/2853 modernizes the EU product-liability framework, including treatment of software and evidence-related issues. Member States must transpose it by 9 December 2026. Following the published corrigendum, the national measures apply to products placed on the market or put into service after 8 December 2026.

Manufacturers should align product identity, software/change history, warnings, safety information, test records and retention rules with the project’s legal review.

Sources: Directive (EU) 2024/2853 and official corrigendum.

20 January 2027

EU Machinery Regulation applies

Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 replaces the Machinery Directive for in-scope products from 20 January 2027, reflecting the corrected application date. Transition plans should assign the applicable regime by product configuration and planned placing-on-market date.

Technical teams should review requirements mapping, risk assessment, instructions and declarations, and assess implications for software, safety-related control functions and digital documentation where relevant.

Sources: Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 and its official corrigendum.

11 December 2027

Cyber Resilience Act full application

The main CRA provisions become fully applicable from 11 December 2027. For in-scope products with digital elements, manufacturers need lifecycle cybersecurity evidence, a cybersecurity risk assessment, vulnerability handling, support-period information, technical documentation and the applicable conformity procedure.

The conformity route can vary by product category and use of harmonised standards or other permitted means. Do not assume self-assessment without a product-specific review.

Source: European Commission — summary of the CRA legislative text.
