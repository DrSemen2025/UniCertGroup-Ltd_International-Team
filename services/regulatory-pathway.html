Service 01

Regulatory pathway review.

Establish the facts and working route before investing in a full documentation or testing program.

Questions the review addresses

  • What product and configurations are being placed on the market?
  • What is the intended use and reasonably foreseeable misuse?
  • Which economic operator places the product on the destination market?
  • Which legislation or technical regulations appear applicable?
  • Is self-assessment available, or is a qualified third party required?
  • Which evidence and standards strategy should be validated next?

The output is a documented working pathway. It is not a formal authority decision or legal opinion.

Core inputs

  • Product description and variants
  • Intended-use statement
  • Target countries and dates
  • Manufacturer/importer information
  • Existing drawings and risk records
  • Current certificates and test reports

Example: machinery

Machinery scope needs more than a CE label decision.

The review considers whether the item is machinery, a related product or an assembly; which other Union legislation may apply; who completes integration; and whether the planned date falls under Directive 2006/42/EC or Regulation (EU) 2023/1230.

For products planned around the January 2027 transition, document planning should address risk-assessment structure, digital instructions where used, software-related safety functions and any applicable third-party route.

Primary reference: Regulation (EU) 2023/1230. Confirm the consolidated text and corrigenda when setting project dates.
