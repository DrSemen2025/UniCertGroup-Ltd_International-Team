Service 02

Technical documentation readiness.

Create a traceable evidence structure that reviewers can navigate, interrogate and maintain.

Evidence areas

  • Product definition, variants and design drawings
  • Applicable requirements and standards mapping
  • Risk assessment and risk-reduction evidence
  • Design calculations and material specifications
  • Verification, validation, inspection and test records
  • Instructions, markings, declarations and change control

The assessment records whether each item is applicable, available, current, approved, traceable and consistent with the product configuration.

Typical outputs

  • Document index and evidence matrix
  • Critical-gap register
  • Standards-edition review list
  • Revision and traceability findings
  • Submission-folder structure
  • Reviewer question log

Explosion protection

ATEX documentation readiness.

For equipment intended for potentially explosive atmospheres, the working file may need product classification, equipment group and category, ignition-hazard assessment, protection concepts, drawings, critical component evidence, markings, instructions and quality-assurance records.

The applicable conformity route and notified-body involvement depend on the product and category. No certificate outcome can be inferred from a preliminary document review.

Primary reference: Directive 2014/34/EU.

Pressure equipment

Pressure-equipment evidence.

A readiness file commonly addresses design basis, fluid and category assumptions, materials, permanent joining, personnel approvals where required, non-destructive examination, pressure tests, safety accessories, instructions and conformity records.

The EU PED route and an ASME code route are distinct; one does not automatically substitute for the other.

Primary reference: Directive 2014/68/EU.
