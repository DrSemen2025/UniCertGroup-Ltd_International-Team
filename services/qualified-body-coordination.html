Service 03

Qualified-body coordination.

Prepare the evidence, questions and decision trail for an efficient interface with the body authorized for the selected route.

Coordination scope

  1. Confirm the required body type and scope of competence.
  2. Prepare the application data and controlled document register.
  3. Record open technical questions and responsibility for responses.
  4. Coordinate samples, test plans or assessment milestones where scoped.
  5. Track findings, revisions and final controlled records.

Selection must consider the body’s actual accreditation, notification or authorization scope—not only its general marketing description.

Independence statement

Unless a written project document expressly states otherwise, UniCertGroup Ltd. International Team acts as a preparation and coordination service. The independent body retains its own contractual, technical and decision-making responsibilities.

No website wording guarantees appointment, test acceptance, approval or certificate issuance.

Due diligence

Checks before relying on a third party.

Identity and authority

Legal identity, authorization database entry, scope, location and current status.

Technical fit

Product category, test method, standard edition, exclusions and subcontracting.

Commercial clarity

Deliverables, timelines, samples, review cycles, confidentiality and change costs.
