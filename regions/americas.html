Americas

Standards and acceptance planning by jurisdiction.

The United States, Canada and Latin American markets use distinct legal, workplace, electrical and sector-specific acceptance mechanisms.

United States

US planning

Review federal and state requirements, OSHA workplace rules, NRTL needs, sector regulators and applicable consensus standards. CE marking does not establish US acceptance.

Canada

Canada planning

Assess federal and provincial requirements, electrical/product certification, pressure-equipment registration and language obligations as applicable.

Latin America

Country-specific planning

Confirm each destination’s competent authority, mandatory standards, local representative, registration and labeling requirements.

Evidence strategy

International test reports can be valuable inputs, but the receiving scheme may require an accepted laboratory, national deviations, a different standard edition, factory inspection or local certification. Record acceptance before scheduling the launch.

Primary starting points: US OSHA NRTL Program, Standards Council of Canada, and the current competent authority for each Latin American destination.
