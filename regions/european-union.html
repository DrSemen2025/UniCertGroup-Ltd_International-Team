European Union

EU conformity and documentation readiness.

Identify applicable Union legislation, the economic operator’s duties, the conformity route and the evidence needed before placing a product on the market.

Core planning questions

  • Which Union harmonisation legislation applies to the product?
  • Who is the manufacturer, importer, distributor or authorized representative?
  • Does the route allow internal production control or require a notified body?
  • Which harmonised standards or other technical specifications support the design?
  • Do the technical file, instructions, markings and declaration agree?
  • Which transitional date controls the planned placing on the market?

Selected frameworks

  • Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU
  • Low Voltage and EMC Directives
  • Radio Equipment Directive
