Eurasian Economic Union

EAEU technical-regulation readiness.

Screen the applicable technical regulations, conformity form, applicant requirements and evidence package before engaging an authorized body.

Working scope

  • Product classification and HS/EAEU nomenclature inputs
  • Potentially applicable EAEU technical regulations
  • Certificate or declaration route and selected scheme
  • Applicant and authorized representative requirements
  • Test evidence, manuals, safety justification and drawings
  • Authorized-body and register checks

EAC marking is not a standalone service label. It follows the successful completion of the applicable conformity procedure and related conditions.

Common industrial starting points

  • TR CU 010/2011 — machinery and equipment safety
  • TR CU 012/2011 — equipment for explosive atmospheres
  • TR CU 032/2013 — pressure equipment safety
  • TR CU 004/2011 — low-voltage equipment
  • TR CU 020/2011 — electromagnetic compatibility

Authority and register verification

Use the Eurasian Economic Commission and the relevant national authority or official register to verify current technical-regulation texts, adopted amendments and the status and scope of the selected conformity-assessment body.

Some official register interfaces may block automated checks or change addresses. A failed automated request should trigger manual verification, not an unsupported conclusion that a certificate or body is invalid.

Primary starting point: Eurasian Economic Commission technical regulations. Confirm current national register access with the project’s applicant jurisdiction.
