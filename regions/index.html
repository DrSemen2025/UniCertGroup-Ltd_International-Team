No single “global certificate.”

Trade groups and regional labels do not automatically create one approval route. BRICS+, Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and the Americas contain separate legal systems and competent authorities. A multi-country program should maintain one product evidence core plus a controlled delta for each destination.

Recommended starting set: product definition, bill of materials, intended-use statement, risk records, drawings, test reports, current certificates, target markets and planned dates.
